# ReadTree

**An open research reading framework for building literature knowledge trees.**

ReadTree is an open framework for organizing academic literature around research questions, theoretical developments, identification strategies, and empirical evidence.

Instead of treating papers as isolated reading materials, ReadTree aims to connect them into a structured and continuously evolving knowledge tree.

## Core Principles

- **Research-question centered:** Organize papers by the questions they address, not just keywords.
- **Literature connections:** Track theoretical foundations, methodological extensions, empirical evidence, and disagreements.
- **Evidence verification:** Check bibliographic details, research designs, and findings against reliable academic sources.
- **Deduplication:** Identify previously reviewed papers, including working-paper and published versions.
- **Cumulative learning:** Build a long-term literature knowledge base through regular reading.

## Initial Research Areas

The first version focuses on economics, including human capital, early childhood development, education economics, intergenerational mobility, gender inequality, family economics, and social policy.

## Project Status

**Version 0.1 — Early framework development**

ReadTree currently provides a proposed research-reading structure and workflow. Automated literature retrieval, persistent deduplication, and interactive knowledge-tree visualization are planned features, not yet implemented.

## Contributing
